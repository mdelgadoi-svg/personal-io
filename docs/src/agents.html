The agents: what each one sees, remembers and does

Personal IO v2.5 · only two parts act on their own: the Follower and the goal picker. The rest are single helpers.

The Follower

The main agent · plain rules · no AI · free
Its jobWatch your whole day, and walk you from play back to the plan, kindly, before play turns into hours. It does not tell you to go away: it changes how the game plays.
What it watchesWhich section you are in and for how long · minutes played today and your last results · the next task and its time · meetings now or within 45 min · a planned task whose time already began · whether you started something.
Its memoryThe moments list: one record per game, start, break, Pause answer, nudge and visit. Saved in this browser and in your Google Drive copy, so phone and computer share it.
Harness
(what wakes it)		A timer every 15 seconds while the app is on screen followTick, app.js · every second on the game screens play.js · after every game. It sleeps when the app is hidden.
Its instructionsFixed rules in js/rules.js (tested): playMode, chessLevel, sudokuSize, playLine, planLine, followerChat, playStats, followerSummary. The numbers: tight = meeting now, something starts within 45 min, a planned task is late, tasks due with no plan, or 30+ min of play today. Otherwise free.
What it doesTight: strong chess opponent (fast game), quick 6x6 Sudoku. Free: easy opponent, full 9x9 grid; 2 wins in a row make it even, 3 make it strong. · The Follower box: time played, "Next: X at 3:00 PM, in 40 min", Start 1 minute, Plan my play. · One kind line at 25 and 45 min of play. · "You have planned enough" after 10 min planning (20 in Life Planning). · Logs what you did after each line. · A short weekly summary you can copy for an AI (Settings, Your numbers).
What it does NOT doBlock anything · send phone notifications · read email · use AI · see anything outside the app (Instagram time you note by hand) · learn by itself (the numbers only change when we change them).

How it decides, step by step

  1. Every 15 seconds it reads your day: the calendar, today's plan, the clock and today's moments.
  2. It calls the day tight or free (the rule above).
  3. You open Procrastination: the Follower box shows time played and what is next, and the game you start gets its level from tight or free (and your win streak).
  4. While you play, every second it updates the box and the chess clock; after 5 minutes without a tap it stops counting your minutes.
  5. A game ends: it saves the result, then shows your next task with Start 1 minute. At 25 and 45 minutes of play today it adds one kind line (once each).
  6. You tap Start 1 minute, Plan my play or Keep playing: it saves which one, for your 2-week test.
  7. In Today's Planning, 10 minutes without starting anything: one line, "You have planned enough. Start the first thing?", for 20 seconds.
Where it could grow (not built): read the weekly summary with Claude for a short personal note; learn your own numbers (when you usually come back); a phone notification when a planned break ends without the app open (today Google Calendar rings instead).

The goal picker

AI agent · Claude, your key · small cost per call
Its jobGive each task its "goal: #" when you did not pick one.
WatchesOpen tasks with no goal picked (or a deleted goal).
Sends to ClaudeOnly task names and goal names with their "why". Nothing else. ai.js pickGoals · model claude-sonnet-5-5 · low effort
Instructions"Sort a person's tasks into his goals. For each task, pick the one goal it clearly serves. If a task could fit two goals equally, or none, answer none. Do not guess." The answer must be a strict list: task id + one of your goal ids or "none".
HarnessfillGoals() after the screen redraws: at most once a minute, only online and with your key, up to 20 tasks per call, each task asked once per name. A refused key: one message, then it stops.
MemoryOn each task: goalBy (me / ai / none) and goalAsked. Key stays on this device.
Does NOTOverride your own pick · change anything else · run without a key (then tasks show "?").
